## Answer

The standard Optional Practical Training (OPT) duration is a year, although extensions are possible under certain circumstances [S2]. For STEM students, there is an extension option that allows foreign STEM students to work in the U.S. for up to 29 months on a student visa [S4]. Furthermore, certain F-1 students who receive STEM degrees and meet other requirements can apply for a 24-month extension of their post-completion OPT, resulting in a total of 36 months of OPT [S6].
