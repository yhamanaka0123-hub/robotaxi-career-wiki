usage: wiki [-h] {chat,ask,search,ingest,status} ...

Personal wiki CLI backed by a local Gemma model (gemma4:e2b via Ollama).

modes:
  chat    personal assistant "Navi": conversation memory, looks up notes only when needed
  ask     standalone factual answer from retrieved passages, with citations
  search  original matching passages and paths; no model answer is generated

  status  show model, runtime, index, and vault state

  wiki ingest ./vault/raw
  wiki search "H-1B annual cap"
  wiki ask "Which company owns Zoox?" --mode local
  wiki chat

  WIKI_VECTOR_WEIGHT   embedding weight in fusion (default 2.0)

  {chat,ask,search,ingest,status}
    ingest              build wiki notes and the search index from sources
    search              show matching original passages (no generation)
    ask                 standalone cited answer from your sources
    chat                talk with Navi, your personal assistant

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
