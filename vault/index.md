## Industry
_The robotaxi market as a whole_

- [[Robotaxi Industry]] — A robotaxi is an autonomous car (SAE level 4 or 5) operated by a ridesharing company.

## Companies
_Robotaxi companies I follow or might apply to_

- [[Waymo]] — Waymo is an American autonomous driving technology company headquartered in Mountain View, California, that develops and operates public commercial robotaxi services.
- [[Zoox]] — Zoox is an American technology company developing driverless, battery-electric vehicles for the robotaxi market.

## Visas
_US work authorization rules that shape my job search_

- [[H-1B Visa]] — The H-1B is a non-immigrant visa that allows U.S. employers to hire foreign workers in specialty occupations.
- [[Optional Practical Training]] — Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows F-1 students to work for one year to gain practical training that complements their education in the United States.
