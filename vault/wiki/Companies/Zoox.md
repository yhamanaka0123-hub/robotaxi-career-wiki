# Zoox

Zoox is an American technology company developing driverless, battery-electric vehicles for the robotaxi market. The company focuses on autonomous, symmetrical, and bi-directional vehicles, aiming to provide mobility as a service. It matters because it is developing autonomous transportation technology and has navigated regulatory and safety challenges.

## Key facts

- Zoox, Inc. is an American technology company subsidiary of Amazon developing driverless vehicles that provide mobility as a service. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Introduction
- Zoox is headquartered in Foster City, California. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Introduction
- Zoox has offices of operations in the San Francisco Bay Area and Seattle. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Introduction
- Zoox is part of the Amazon Devices & Services organization along with other Amazon units like Amazon Lab126, Amazon Alexa, and Amazon Leo. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Introduction
- As of January 2026, Zoox has approximately 50 robotaxis in service. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Services
- Zoox was founded in 2014 by Australian artist-designer Tim Kentley-Klay and Jesse Levinson. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § History
- Zoox became the first company to gain approval for providing self-driving transport services to the public in California in December 2018. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Progress and competition
- Zoox had raised $800 million in venture capital and had a valuation of $3.2 billion by July 2018. — [[raw/Zoox - Wikipedia|Zoox - Wikipedia]] § Progress and competition
